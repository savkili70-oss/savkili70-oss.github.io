Privacy Policy - Umbrift

Developer: SVKL Studio · Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Umbrift (package name com.svklstudio.umbrift, the "Game") by SVKL Studio ("we", "us") handles information. We designed the Game to collect as little data as possible.

1. Summary

  • No account, sign-up or login is required.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • We do not collect, store or sell your personal information on our own servers.
  • The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID and similar data as described below.

2. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (levels completed, stars, gold, unlocked heroes, upgrades, potions) and settings (language, sound, music) in local storage on your device. This data is never sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the Game. The Game works fully offline.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and contains optional advertising served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the SDK may collect and process, depending on your device, region and consent choices:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • Ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and app interaction information;
  • Diagnostics, crash and performance data;
  • Basic device and app information (such as device model, OS version, app version).

This data is collected and processed by Google and its advertising partners under Google's own policies; we do not receive your personal data from it. You may see personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your choices.

Useful links:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure
  • Google My Ad Center (manage ad personalization)

4. Consent and your choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads are personalized. You can change or withdraw your consent where the Game offers a privacy options entry, and you can always:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads;
  • Opt out of ad personalization in the same menu or in Google My Ad Center;
  • Uninstall the Game to stop all data processing by the Game.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data ourselves. The only third party that processes data through the Game is Google (AdMob) as described above.

6. Security and retention

Data from the ad SDK is transmitted over encrypted connections. Local save data stays on your device until you delete it. We hold no user data on our servers, so we have no data to retain or delete on our side.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is not designed for or directed at children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country), and it contains fantasy cartoon violence. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help. Parents can also limit ad personalization through their child's Google account and device settings.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold your personal data, requests about ad data should be directed to Google through the links above. You may contact us with any privacy question.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game adds features. The latest version and its date will always be shown on this page. Continued use of the Game after changes means you accept the updated policy.
