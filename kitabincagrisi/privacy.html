Privacy Policy - Kitabın Çağrısı

Developer: SVKL Studio · Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information Kitabın Çağrısı ("the Book's Call"), an online book club by SVKL Studio ("we"), collects and how it is used. It covers the website https://kitabincagrisi.online and the Android app (package com.svklstudio.kitabincagrisi), together "the Service".

1. Summary

  • You create an account to join (email/password or Google sign-in).
  • Content you post (comments, reviews, quotes, posts, suggestions) is visible to other members.
  • No ads, no sale of data, no advertising tracking.
  • You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

2. Information we collect

  • Account: email address, display name and, with Google sign-in, a link to your profile photo. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot see them.
  • Profile (optional): short bio, favourite genres, city, website, reading goal, members you follow or block.
  • Your content: chapter comments, reviews and reading reports, ratings, quotes, community posts and replies, book suggestions, poll votes, likes and content you report.
  • Reading data: your shelf of read books, reading progress, points and badges.
  • On your device: theme preference and an offline cache. These are not sent to us.

We do not request location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.

3. How we use it

To create and run your account and profile, operate club features (weekly book, discussions, polls, points and badges), prevent abuse and review reported content, and send service emails such as password resets and email verification.

4. Who can see it

Your display name, photo, profile details, shelf and posted content are public on club pages. Your email address is never shown to other members.

5. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) stores account and content data. Firebase privacy.
  • Open Library / Internet Archive provide book covers and details; your IP address is sent to them when these load.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs serve fonts and icons.
  • Web hosting: our hosting provider may keep standard access logs (IP address, time).

We do not share or sell data to anyone else.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is open. Use Settings → Delete my account in the app to permanently delete your profile, shelf and the posts, quotes, comments, reviews and suggestions you made, and close your sign-in account. If you cannot access the app, follow the steps on the account deletion page. Backup copies are removed within 30 days.

7. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Database rules ensure each member can only change their own data.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Contact us and we will delete such an account.

9. Your rights

Under GDPR, KVKK and similar laws you may access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data. You can edit or delete your profile in the app; email us for anything else.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version and date are always on this page.
