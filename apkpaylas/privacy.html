Privacy Policy - APK Share

Developer: SVKL Studio · Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the APK Share Android app (package name com.svklstudio.apkpaylas, the "App") by SVKL Studio ("we") handles information.

1. Summary

  • The App does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
  • There are no ads, analytics, trackers or third-party SDKs.
  • No account, sign-up or login is required.
  • No storage, location, contacts or camera permission is requested.

2. App list

To show you which apps can be shared, the App reads the name, icon, version and size of launchable apps on your device through Android's standard package information API. This information is only displayed on screen; it is never sent anywhere or saved.

3. Sharing and temporary files

When you share an app, its installation file (APK, or .apks for split packages) is prepared in the App's own temporary cache folder. The file only goes where you choose:

  • Send: to the app you pick in the Android share menu (WhatsApp, Quick Share, Bluetooth, etc.). From then on, that app's own privacy policy applies.
  • Download via QR: the App opens a temporary download address on your local network only (Wi-Fi or hotspot). The address contains a random key and stops working when you close the screen. No data goes to the internet or to our servers.

Temporary files are deleted when the QR screen closes and every time the App starts.

4. Network permission

The network (internet) permission is used only for the local QR download server described above. The App does not connect to any server on the internet by itself. If you tap the "Privacy policy" link, this page opens in your browser.

5. Responsible use

Only share apps you have the right to share. For paid or licensed apps, use the Google Play link the App offers. Only install files from people you trust; you can verify a file with the SHA-256 value the App shows.

6. Children's privacy

Since the App collects no personal data, it collects none from children either. The App is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children.

7. Your rights

We hold no personal data, so there is nothing of yours for us to access, correct or delete. You can write to us with any privacy question.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when new features are added. The latest version and its date are always shown on this page.
